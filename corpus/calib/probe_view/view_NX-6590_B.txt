Tool-use benchmarks are evaluated using the standard function call format, wherein models are configured to thinking mode. For MCP-Universe ( Luo et al., 2025 ) and MCP-Mark ( EvalSys, 2025 ) , we evaluate all models with our internal environment, because the search and playwright environment might be slightly different from the official setting. We set the temperature to 1.0, and the context window to 128K tokens. For math-related tasks such as AIME, HMMT, IMOAnswerBench, and HLE, we eval with the following template: "{question}\nPlease reason step by step, and put your final answer within \boxed{}."
